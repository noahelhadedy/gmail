Privacy

Gmail Menu Bar · Last updated 31 October 2026

Gmail Menu Bar is a Mac app that runs on your own computer. There is no server behind it, and the developer never sees your email.

What the app accesses

When you add an account, you sign in with Google and allow the gmail.modify permission. The app uses it to read your inbox's unread count, show the sender, subject, time and a short preview of recent unread emails, and mark an email as read when you ask it to. It doesn't send, delete or change anything else.

Where your data goes

Nowhere except your Mac and Google. Sign-in tokens are stored in your Mac's Keychain. Email details are kept in memory to draw the menu and are not saved to disk, sold, shared, or sent to the developer or anyone else. There are no analytics or ads.

Google API Services

Gmail Menu Bar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Remove an account in the app's settings, or revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app and its Keychain items removes everything it stored.
